Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access Founding Member benefits?

Your Founding Member subscription supports FixWise AI and gives you access to the features currently available on the public site, plus eligibility for future member updates as new features are released.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Your $9.99/month Founding Member subscription is recurring monthly until canceled. You can cancel it through PayPal.

Do I need an account to use the site?

No. The current public site does not provide customer accounts or saved cloud history.

Is FixWise AI a replacement for a contractor?

No. FixWise AI helps you understand problems before calling a pro, but high-risk or complex repairs should be handled by licensed contractors.
