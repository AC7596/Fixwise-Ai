    FixWise Kids

    Fix it together. Learn it together.

    FixWise Kids is built around the idea that a child can safely follow along with the same repair their parent is doing in the real world. Fixy turns the repair into an age-appropriate learning adventure.

    ToolsLearn what tools do and how they are used safely.
    MeasurementsPractice length, size, counting, and basic math.
    Problem-solvingLearn how to test ideas instead of guessing.
    MoneyUnderstand parts, repair costs, saving, and value.
    Safety first

    Some jobs should stop before they start.

    FixWise AI should always flag high-risk situations involving electricity, gas, structural damage, fire, hazardous materials, or other conditions where professional help is the safer choice.
