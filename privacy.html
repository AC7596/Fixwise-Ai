Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

FixWise AI respects your privacy. This policy describes the current data handling of the public FixWise AI static website.

2. Information Stored in Your Browser

The current FixWise AI website uses browser storage on your device to keep parts of the experience working locally. This includes temporary diagnosis session details in sessionStorage for the current tab and selected progress or activity data in localStorage. This information stays in your browser and is not uploaded to FixWise AI by the current static-site implementation.

3. Diagnosis Entries, Photos, and AI Processing

When you use the current public diagnosis tools, the information you type and any photos you select are used in your browser for the on-site experience. The current public GitHub Pages site does not provide user accounts, server-side diagnosis history, or live AI photo analysis. Automatic AI-powered backend processing and cloud-stored features may be added in the future, but they are not active on the current public static site.

4. PayPal Payments

Founding Member subscription payments are handled by PayPal. Payment card details and PayPal billing information are handled by PayPal, not stored by the current FixWise AI static GitHub Pages site, and are subject to PayPal's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Future Features

Based on the current implementation, the public FixWise AI static site does not run its own analytics platform, advertising trackers, or customer account system. If future versions add accounts, backend processing, analytics, or other server-connected features, this Privacy Policy should be updated before those features are offered publicly.

6. Privacy Contact

For privacy questions about the current FixWise AI site or Founding Member subscription, please use the FixWise AI Contact page. Entity/service name: FixWise AI.
